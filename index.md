# Jalin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Jalin is a keyboard for iPhone that types English, Malay and Indonesian, alone or mixed. This policy explains what Jalin does with what you type.

## The short version

Everything Jalin learns stays on your iPhone, or in your own iCloud if you turn sync on. Jalin has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you type leaves your iPhone unless you turn on Online tools and then choose to edit or translate a piece of text (only that text is sent, to the provider you picked, with your own key), or you turn on GIFs and search for a GIF (only the words you search are sent, to KLIPY, and only while GIFs is on).

## What Jalin keeps on your iPhone

- **Words you use.** To suggest and correct better, Jalin remembers words you type, how often you use them, which words follow each other, how you capitalise them, and where your thumbs tend to land on each key. This is stored in a file inside Jalin's own storage on your iPhone.
- **Corrections you undo.** When you undo an autocorrection with backspace, Jalin notes the word and the correction so you can review or share them from Dictionary. They stay on your iPhone unless you share them.
- **Your settings and shortcuts.** Your keyboard settings and any text shortcuts you create are stored the same way.
- **Purchase status.** If you buy or try Jalin Pro, Jalin stores the purchase date and trial dates on your iPhone. The purchase itself is handled by Apple; Jalin never sees your payment details.
- **My info.** If you fill in My info (name, email, phone, address, bank account), it is stored on your iPhone; the bank account in the iPhone keychain. Jalin inserts a value only when you tap it, and the bank account only on a second tap.
- **Clips.** Text is saved as a clip only when you tap Save clip, which reads the clipboard at that moment. Jalin never reads the clipboard for clips otherwise. Up to 20 clips stay on your iPhone (you can raise the limit to 50 or 100 with Keep up to), and you can remove them or turn Keep clips off.
- **Typing statistics and diagnostics.** Counts such as words typed and corrections made. Only if you switch on Keep diagnostics, Jalin also keeps a short log of the keyboard's memory use, and, while Swipe typing and Full Access are on as well, a swipe log: for each of your last 2,000 swipes, the time, the keyboard layout, the path your finger drew, the three words Jalin offered, the word it inserted and the word you kept. Nothing else you type is written to it. All of this stays on your iPhone, and switching Keep diagnostics off deletes both logs.

You can see and delete learned words in the Jalin app under Dictionary, and remove everything by deleting the app. Dictionary › Back up writes a copy of this data to a file only when you ask, to a place you choose.

## iCloud sync

Sync across your iPhones is off until you turn it on in the Jalin app under Typing. When it is on, the words Jalin learned on this iPhone, the word pairs it counted, your blocked words, your never-corrected words as you typed them (with their capitals), the words you deleted or unblocked (so they stay that way on your other iPhones), this iPhone's generic device name and the time of each sync are stored in your own iCloud private database, where only your devices signed in to the same Apple ID can read them; Jalin's developer has no access to them. Tap positions, settings and everything else stay on the iPhone. Turning the switch off deletes this iPhone's copy from iCloud and removes what the other iPhones sent to it.

## What Jalin reads but does not keep

- **Contact names.** iOS gives every keyboard the names of your contacts so they are not "corrected". Jalin uses them only to suggest and spell names. It does not copy, store or send your contacts.
- **iOS text replacements.** iOS gives keyboards your Text Replacements (Settings › General › Keyboard › Text Replacement) so they expand as you type. Jalin uses them only for that.
- **Clipboard.** Jalin checks whether the clipboard has new text so it can offer a Paste button, without reading the text. It reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste or use Copy, Cut or Paste on the keys.

## Full Access

iOS asks whether to "Allow Full Access" for third-party keyboards. Jalin works without it. With Full Access on, Jalin can use haptic feedback, the clipboard features above, share settings between the keyboard and the app, and, only if you turn on Online tools, reach the provider you chose.

## Online tools

Online tools are off until you turn them on in the Jalin app under Tools. They also need Full Access.

- **What is sent.** Only when you tap an AI edit or Translate action: the text you selected or, with nothing selected, the paragraph before the cursor, up to 1,200 characters. Nothing is sent while you type, and nothing at all while Online tools are off.
- **To whom.** The provider you picked: Anthropic (Claude), or the OpenAI-compatible service you configured. GIF search is separate; see below.
- **What is not sent.** Your key is stored in the iPhone keychain and sent only to that provider. Jalin never sends the rest of your document, your contacts or your typing history.
- **The provider's own policy.** What the provider does with the text it receives is covered by that provider's own privacy policy.

## GIF search

GIF search is off until you turn on GIFs in the Jalin app under Tools › GIFs and add your KLIPY key; it also needs Full Access. Only the words you search are sent to KLIPY, and only while GIFs is on and you search in the keyboard's GIF panel (with no words typed, the panel asks KLIPY for what is trending, which sends no words). No identifier for you or your iPhone is sent, and your key is kept in the iPhone keychain and sent only to KLIPY. The GIF you tap is downloaded from KLIPY and put on the clipboard so you can paste it; what KLIPY does with a search is covered by KLIPY's own privacy policy.

## Children

Jalin does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
