# Jalin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Jalin is a keyboard for iPhone that types English, Malay and Indonesian, alone or mixed. This policy explains what Jalin does with what you type.

## The short version

Jalin has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. What Jalin learns is stored on your iPhone. Text you type is sent off your iPhone when you use Online tools (the text you edit or translate, to the provider you picked, with your own key), GIF search (the words you search, to KLIPY) or iCloud sync (what Jalin learns, to your own iCloud); each of these is off until you turn it on. Your iPhone's own backups, and files you share from Dictionary or Diagnostics, include this data too.

## What Jalin keeps on your iPhone

- **Words you use.** To suggest and correct better, Jalin remembers words you type, how often you use them, which words follow each other, how you capitalise them, and where your thumbs tend to land on each key. This is stored in a file inside Jalin's own storage on your iPhone.
- **Corrections you undo.** When you undo an autocorrection and keep the word you typed, Jalin notes the word, the correction and the word before it so you can review or share them from Dictionary. They are stored on your iPhone; you can share them from Dictionary.
- **Your settings and shortcuts.** Your keyboard settings and any text shortcuts you create are stored the same way.
- **Purchase status.** If you buy or try Jalin Pro, Jalin stores the purchase date and trial dates on your iPhone. The purchase itself is handled by Apple; Jalin never sees your payment details.
- **My info.** If you fill in My info (name, email, phone, address, bank account), it is stored on your iPhone; the bank account in the iPhone keychain. Jalin inserts a value only when you tap it, and the bank account only on a second tap.
- **Clips.** Text is saved as a clip only when you tap Save clip, which reads the clipboard at that moment. Jalin never reads the clipboard for clips otherwise. Up to 20 clips are stored on your iPhone (you can raise the limit to 50 or 100 with Keep up to), and you can remove them or turn Keep clips off.
- **Typing statistics and diagnostics.** Counts such as words typed and corrections made. Only if you switch on Keep diagnostics, Jalin also keeps a short log of the keyboard's memory use, and, while Swipe typing and Full Access are on as well, a swipe log: for each of your last 2,000 swipes, the time, the keyboard layout, the path your finger drew, the three words Jalin offered, the word it inserted and the word you kept. Nothing else you type is written to it. All of this is stored on your iPhone, and switching Keep diagnostics off deletes both logs.

When you type a word Jalin does not know and keep it as typed, Jalin may store it from the first time you type it. Jalin skips some words when it learns from typing (for example words with numbers, and words you blocked), but any word you type, including a name or a blocked word, can be counted in the word pairs, and a word you keep after undoing a correction goes on your never-corrected list. The Dictionary does not list a word you have typed only once; such a word is still stored, synced if iCloud sync is on, and included in Dictionary backups. Without Full Access the keyboard keeps these words in its own storage. Words learned on another iPhone through sync are used on this iPhone but are not listed in its Dictionary. You can delete the words the Dictionary lists. Reset learned words clears the words, word pairs, capitals and tap positions this iPhone learned, including words typed once; blocked words, never-corrected words, the corrections you undid and, with sync on, words from your other iPhones stay. Reset also deletes the older copies Jalin keeps for safety in its shared folder and in the app's own storage: the file from before a restore, any file it could not read, and copies of words it already imported. Copies in the keyboard's own container, and words learned while Full Access was off, go only when you delete the app. The lists of words you deleted or unblocked in Dictionary (up to 2,000 each) are kept in the app's settings even when iCloud sync is off, until you teach Jalin the word again while sync is on or delete the app. Deleting the app removes Jalin's files from this iPhone; your bank account and any keys you entered in Jalin stay in the iPhone keychain until you clear them in the app first. Like other app data, Jalin's files are included in your iPhone's own backups. Dictionary › Back up saves a copy of this data to a place you choose; a temporary copy is made when you tap Back up and deleted when the share sheet closes.

The text you send for an AI edit, a translation or a GIF search is held in memory while the keyboard is open and is not saved by Jalin.

## iCloud sync

Sync across your iPhones is off until you turn it on in the Jalin app under Typing. When it is on, the words Jalin learned on this iPhone, the word pairs it counted, your blocked words, your never-corrected words as you typed them (with their capitals), the words you deleted or unblocked (so your other iPhones can drop them too), this iPhone's generic device name, a random ID for this iPhone and the time of each sync are stored in your own iCloud private database, where only your devices signed in to the same Apple ID can read them; Jalin's developer has no access to them. Tap positions and settings are not synced. Turning the switch off removes what the other iPhones sent to this iPhone and asks iCloud to delete this iPhone's copy. Deleting the app does not delete this iPhone's iCloud copy, so turn iCloud sync off first.

## What Jalin reads

- **Contact names.** iOS gives every keyboard the names of your contacts so they are not "corrected". Jalin uses them only to suggest and spell names. It does not copy or send your contact list; a name you type can be counted in the word pairs like any other word.
- **iOS text replacements.** iOS gives keyboards your Text Replacements (Settings › General › Keyboard › Text Replacement) so they expand as you type. Jalin uses them only for that.
- **Clipboard.** Jalin checks whether the clipboard has new text so it can offer a Paste button, without reading the text. It reads the clipboard when you tap Paste, Clean link or Save clip, or the Paste key.

## Full Access

iOS asks whether to "Allow Full Access" for third-party keyboards. Jalin works without it. With Full Access on, Jalin can use haptic feedback and key sounds, the clipboard features above, share settings between the keyboard and the app, and, if you turn on Online tools or GIFs, reach the provider you chose or KLIPY.

## Online tools

Online tools are off until you turn them on in the Jalin app under Tools. They also need Full Access.

- **What is sent.** Only when you tap an AI edit or Translate action: the text you selected or, with nothing selected, the paragraph before the cursor, up to 1,200 characters, with the instruction for the action you tapped (including your own Custom instruction). Nothing is sent while you type, and nothing at all while Online tools are off.
- **To whom.** The provider you picked: Anthropic (Claude), or the OpenAI-compatible service you configured. If you picked On device, the text is handled by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone and no network request is made for it. GIF search is separate; see below.
- **What is not sent.** Your key is stored in the iPhone keychain and sent only to that provider. Jalin never sends the rest of your document, your contacts or your typing history.
- **Test buttons.** In the Jalin app, the Test button for an Online tools key sends the word "ok" to that provider, and the Test button for a KLIPY key asks KLIPY for what is trending. They send nothing you typed.
- **The provider's own policy.** What the provider does with the text it receives is covered by that provider's own privacy policy.

## GIF search

GIF search is off until you turn on GIFs in the Jalin app under Tools › GIFs and add your KLIPY key; it also needs Full Access. Only the words you search are sent to KLIPY, and only while GIFs is on and you search in the keyboard's GIF panel or tap Test in the app (with no words typed, the panel asks KLIPY for what is trending, which sends no words). Jalin does not send KLIPY a customer ID; your key is kept in the iPhone keychain and sent only to KLIPY. The previews shown in the panel and the GIF you tap are downloaded from KLIPY, and the GIF you tap is put on the clipboard so you can paste it. As with any download, KLIPY's servers see your iPhone's IP address and Jalin's name and version; what KLIPY does with a search is covered by KLIPY's own privacy policy.

The Jalin app sends nothing to its developer.
