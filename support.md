# Jalin Support

Jalin is an iPhone keyboard for English, Malay and Indonesian.

## Report a problem

1. During testing: in the TestFlight app, open Jalin and tap Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot while using Jalin and choose Share Beta Feedback.
2. App Store version: use Report a Problem on Jalin's App Store page or in your Apple purchase history.

## What to include

- What you typed
- What Jalin did
- What you expected
- The app it happened in
- Your iPhone model and iOS version

Jalin has no account and no support email; the developer reads the reports sent this way.
